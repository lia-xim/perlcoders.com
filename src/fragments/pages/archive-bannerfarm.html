Known security advisories

Advisories on record

None located

No advisory has been located in our sources. Click-counting scripts of this era frequently wrote to flat files without locking and redirected to an unvalidated target parameter — an open-redirect shape. Both are worth assuming until shown otherwise.

05

Modern equivalent pattern

If the requirement is attribution rather than advertising, log server-side against an allow-list of destinations and redirect only to entries on that list. An open redirect is not a feature you get to keep for convenience.
