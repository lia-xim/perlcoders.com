WeddingRegistry

An online gift-registry script from the CGI era. The archive records what it did without restoring a store, account system or database. Nothing is available here.

01

Documented purpose

A dated 2003 capture describes a MySQL-backed registry where guests could claim gifts without two people selecting the same item. It also mentions sending email to guests. Those are historical product claims, not current capabilities.

02

Why it stays offline

Data and identity boundaryA registry processes names, email addresses and personal event information. No customer data, consent record, security assessment or service relationship transferred with the domain.
