How an acquired Perl domain became a useful site again.

This is not a story about transferring old authority to a new sales page. It is the record of how we researched a legacy domain, separated history from new authorship and rebuilt it around a useful continuation of the topic.

Starting point

What we actually acquired.

The acquisition covered the domain name. It did not transfer the former company, authors, software, customers, accounts, support duties, branding or archive rights.

Historic topic
Perl and CGI tutorials, scripts and software resources.

Method

Five decisions made the rebuild defensible.

  1. 01Research the former purposeUse dated archives and live external references as evidence, never as permission to republish.
  2. 02Classify material URLs individuallyMatch intent only where the successor genuinely answers the old job.
  3. 03Write new, useful contentNo former downloads, screenshots, customers or authors are presented as current assets.
  4. 04Make uncertainty visibleUntested examples, missing review and incomplete historical evidence stay labelled.
  5. 05Test the releaseCanonicals, sitemap, redirects, 404/410, mobile layouts, keyboard use, console errors and accessibility are part of the release gate.
