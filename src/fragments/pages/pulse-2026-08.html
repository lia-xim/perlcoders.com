Editorial

What changed, and who we are not

PerlCoders relaunched under new ownership in 2026. We are not the former PerlCoders company, we do not claim its credits or its clients, and we do not sell or support its software. The Archive exists so that inbound links from the old catalogue land somewhere honest instead of a 404 or a sales page.
