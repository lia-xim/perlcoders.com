410

Gone, and not coming back.

410 rather than 404 is deliberate. A 404 says “not here right now”; a 410 says “this will never be here again”, which is both true and more useful — to you, and to anything still crawling twenty-year-old links.

Why this class of URL is permanently gone These are endpoints of a company that no longer exists, or executable surfaces that should never be restored. Redirecting them somewhere plausible would be worse than admitting they are gone.

If you were looking for the software itself

It is not available here and we do not hold a copy. The Archive records what each product was called, which URLs pointed at it and what we can honestly say about what it did — and nothing more. That is the whole offer.

If you maintain something like this

Script Rescue reads a period CGI script line by line and rewrites it, and the Legacy URL Mapper exists for exactly the job of deciding which of your old URLs should 301, which should 404 and which should land here.
