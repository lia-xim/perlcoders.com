TotalAVS Pro

A Perl CGI script formerly distributed by the original PerlCoders company. Recorded here because the legacy URL — which contains an encoded space — still appears in archived indexes and needs a stable destination. It is not available, not supported, and not recommended for use.

03

Historical URL variants

The space in the product name was encoded two different ways in the wild. Both forms normalise to the same identifier and both resolve here.

  • /main/scripts.html?script=TotalAVS%20Pro
  • /main/scripts.html?script=TotalAVS+Pro

This entry is the reason the resolver normalises rather than matching literally: percent-encoding, plus-encoding and case all vary across twenty years of inbound links, and three near-identical strings pointing at one resource is a routing problem, not a content problem.
