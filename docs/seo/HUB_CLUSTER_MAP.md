## Internal-link rules

- A child links to its parent hub and to one or two genuinely adjacent pages.
- Labs links connect tool, exact rules and measurement report; Archive links connect action method and named records.
- Archive records do not link to unrelated commercial pages.
- The existing Contextter disclosure remains confined to the methods evidence where Contextter was actually used; no footer or reciprocal network is added.
- Search and error surfaces are navigation aids, not index targets.
